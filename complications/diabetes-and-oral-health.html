Diabetes And Oral Health

Category: Complications • By Editorial Team • Updated 2026-09-18 • 2 min read

Advertisement

Why diabetes affects the mouth

Elevated glucose raises glucose in saliva, which changes the environment of the mouth and the risk of gum problems.

This is why gum disease is more common and more severe in people with diabetes.

Why the connection runs both ways

Gum inflammation can worsen insulin resistance, which means oral health affects glucose as well as the reverse.

This is why dental care is part of diabetes care, not separate from it.

The practical routine

Brushing twice daily, cleaning between the teeth, and attending dental checkups on schedule.

Report bleeding gums or loose teeth rather than assuming they are minor.

What to ask your dentist and clinician

Mention your diabetes and medications at every dental visit, and ask how often checkups are needed.

Some dental procedures require coordination with your medical team.
