Diabetes And Heart Health

Category: Complications • By Editorial Team • Updated 2026-09-18 • 1 min read

Advertisement

Why the heart is the priority

Diabetes substantially raises cardiovascular risk over years, and this is the most common serious long-term outcome.

That sounds alarming, but the protective levers are well established and highly effective.

The three things that matter most

Blood pressure control, cholesterol management, and glucose control, in that order for most people.

This is why your clinician pays attention to all three rather than glucose alone.

What actually reduces risk

Taking prescribed medication, staying active, not smoking, and attending every screening.

None is dramatic, and together they reduce risk substantially over years.

The symptoms that warrant urgent attention

Chest pain or pressure, breathlessness, an irregular or rapid heartbeat, and pain in the arm or jaw.

These are not symptoms to discuss at the next appointment.

"Blood pressure control, cholesterol management, and glucose control, in that order for most people."

Bring these details to your next visit

  • Know your blood pressure and cholesterol targets.
  • Take all prescribed medication as directed.
  • Attend every scheduled heart and kidney screening.
  • Report chest pain or breathlessness urgently.
  • Discuss any medication side effects rather than stopping.
  • Ask about safe activity levels for you.
