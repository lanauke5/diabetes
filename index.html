Important health and emergency notice

This website provides general education, not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or an emergency service. It cannot account for your symptoms, medical history, medicines, pregnancy, lab results, or personal glucose targets. Do not start, stop, or change insulin, diabetes medicine, supplements, monitoring, or a care plan based on this site.

Discuss personal decisions with a qualified healthcare professional who knows your history. If you have severe symptoms, loss of consciousness, trouble breathing, signs of dangerously high or low blood sugar, or believe you may have an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately. Do not wait for a website, app, or device reading. Read the full medical disclaimer.
