Prediabetes Blood Sugar Levels

Category: Prediabetes • By Editorial Team • Updated 2026-09-18 • 1 min read

Advertisement

The ranges usually used

Fasting glucose in a specific elevated-but-not-diabetic range, and HbA1c in a similar intermediate band.

The exact thresholds vary slightly by guideline, which is why your clinician interprets them for you.

What a number in that range means

It signals reduced insulin sensitivity, not a disease state with complications.

It is an early warning, which is exactly why it is useful rather than alarming.

What one number cannot tell you

A single result does not establish a trend. Readings fluctuate with illness, sleep, and stress.

Confirmation and the pattern over time are what matter.

The question to ask

Ask what your numbers are, what they mean for you specifically, and when to retest.

A number without context produces either false reassurance or unnecessary fear.

"It signals reduced insulin sensitivity, not a disease state with complications."

Bring these details to your next visit

  • Ask your clinician for your actual numbers.
  • Ask which guideline and ranges are being used.
  • Request confirmation if only one test was done.
  • Ask when to retest and why.
  • Discuss what would change the plan.
  • Bring your questions to the next visit.
