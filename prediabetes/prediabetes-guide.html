What prediabetes is

Glucose higher than typical but not in the diabetes range, usually reflecting reduced insulin sensitivity.

It means risk is elevated, not that diabetes is certain.

Why early identification matters

This is the stage where intervention has the widest range of options and the clearest evidence of benefit.

Knowing gives you time, which is the main advantage.

What actually reduces progression

Sustained activity, dietary change, adequate sleep, and modest weight loss where appropriate.

These reduce progression risk substantially, and they work at any age.

The mindset that helps

Treat it as information to act on, not a label to fear. Panic leads to extreme plans that do not last.

Small changes sustained for years are what change outcomes.

"This is the stage where intervention has the widest range of options and the clearest evidence of benefit."

Bring these details to your next visit

  • Ask for your numbers and what they mean.
  • Get a written retesting schedule.
  • Establish one sustainable activity habit.
  • Reduce refined and liquid carbohydrate.
  • Ask whether weight loss is appropriate for you.
  • Treat it as a signal to act on, not a label.
