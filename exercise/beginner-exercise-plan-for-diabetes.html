Beginner Exercise Plan For Diabetes

Category: Exercise • By Editorial Team • Updated 2026-09-18 • 2 min read

Advertisement

Week one: establish the habit

Ten minutes of walking a day, at any pace, on five days. The goal is to make the session unremarkable.

Same time each day works best, because the existing routine does the reminding.

Weeks two and three: build gradually

Add five minutes every few days, and keep the frequency at five days rather than extending single sessions.

A weekly total of 60 to 90 minutes is a strong foundation by week three.

Week four: add variety

Try one session of something different: cycling, swimming, or a short strength routine.

This is when a second activity type becomes appealing rather than intimidating.

What to do when the plan breaks

Missing a week is not starting over. Resume at the last comfortable level and continue.

The single most useful skill is returning without judgment.

Bring these details to your next visit

  • Fix a daily time for the first two weeks.
  • Start at ten minutes and add five every few days.
  • Keep five days a week rather than extending sessions.
  • Check glucose around the first few sessions.
  • Add one different activity in week four.
  • Resume at the last comfortable level after a break.
