Strength Training And Diabetes

Category: Exercise • By Editorial Team • Updated 2026-09-18 • 2 min read

Advertisement

Why muscle matters for glucose

Muscle takes up a large share of circulating glucose, and more muscle means more capacity to clear it.

This is why resistance training improves insulin sensitivity even without weight loss.

How much is needed

Two sessions a week covering the main movement patterns is enough for most people.

Sessions do not need to be long; consistency and progression matter more than duration.

Getting started safely

Bodyweight movements, resistance bands, or light weights all work. The first weeks are about learning the movement.

A single session with a qualified instructor prevents months of inefficient or unsafe practice.

Progression without injury

Add difficulty gradually, keep rest days between sessions, and stop any exercise that causes sharp or joint pain.

Soreness is normal; joint pain is not.

"Two sessions a week covering the main movement patterns is enough for most people."

Bring these details to your next visit

  • Aim for two sessions a week covering main movements.
  • Start with bodyweight, bands, or light weights.
  • Consider one session with a qualified instructor.
  • Keep a rest day between sessions.
  • Stop for sharp or joint pain, not soreness.
  • Check glucose around the first few sessions.
