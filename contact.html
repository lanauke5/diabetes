The right details help us review your note

NaturalHealthOne welcomes clear, specific feedback. We cannot provide individual diagnosis, treatment recommendations, prescription guidance, or emergency support.

Editorial corrections

Include the page title or URL, the passage at issue, why it may be inaccurate, and a reliable source when available. Our approach to corrections is described in the Editorial Policy.

Medical review

For questions about review standards, identify the article and claim. See the Medical Review Policy and Medical Review Board.

Privacy or accessibility

Describe the page, device or browser, and the result you expected. Do not send account credentials, medical records, insurance details, or payment information.

Tell us which page you visited and what would make it clearer or easier to use. Product pitches and requests for individualized health advice are outside this channel.
