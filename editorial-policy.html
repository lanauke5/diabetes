Our Standards

Editorial Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

At Natural Health One, accuracy and trust are not optional — they are the foundation of everything we publish. This page explains exactly how our content is created, reviewed, and maintained.

✍️

Expert-Written

Content is written by qualified medical writers, nutritionists, and diabetes educators.

🩺

Medically Reviewed

All clinical content is reviewed and approved by board-certified physicians.

🔄

Regularly Updated

We audit and update articles to reflect current medical evidence and guidelines.

Our Mission

Natural Health One is committed to providing people living with Type 2 Diabetes, Prediabetes, and related metabolic conditions with accurate, accessible, and actionable health information. We believe that well-informed patients make better decisions — and that better decisions lead to better health outcomes.

We do not sensationalize, exaggerate, or oversimplify complex medical topics. Our goal is to be the most trustworthy diabetes resource on the internet, not the most viral.

Who Creates Our Content

All articles, guides, and reviews are written by professionals with relevant expertise. Our author team includes registered dietitians (RDs), certified diabetes care and education specialists (CDCES), medical writers with clinical backgrounds, and journalists who specialize in health and medicine.

Every author profile on our site displays their credentials, areas of expertise, and a link to their full biography. You can meet our full team on the Authors page.

The Medical Review Process

Before any article containing clinical or medical claims is published, it is reviewed by at least one member of our Medical Review Board — a panel of board-certified physicians and specialists including endocrinologists, cardiologists, and registered dietitians.

Reviewers check for clinical accuracy, up-to-date guidelines alignment, and appropriate nuance. They also flag any language that could be interpreted as a diagnosis or a specific treatment recommendation. For details on how our review process works, see our Medical Review Policy.

How We Source Information

We rely exclusively on high-quality, peer-reviewed sources. Our citation hierarchy is as follows:

  1. Primary research — Randomized controlled trials (RCTs) and systematic reviews published in peer-reviewed journals.
  2. Clinical guidelines — Standards of Care from the American Diabetes Association (ADA), World Health Organization (WHO), and equivalent national bodies.
  3. Secondary sources — Reputable medical institutions such as Mayo Clinic, Cleveland Clinic, and NIH, used only when primary sources are not available.
  4. Expert opinion — Statements from our Medical Review Board, used to contextualize complex data for a general audience.

We do not cite anecdotal evidence, press releases, or commercial studies without independent corroboration. For more detail, see our Fact-Checking Policy.

Content Updates & Review Cycles

Health information evolves. What was considered best practice five years ago may no longer reflect current evidence. We are committed to keeping our content current through a formal review schedule:

  • High-priority clinical content (e.g., medication, A1C targets, treatment protocols): reviewed every 12 months or when major guidelines change.
  • Lifestyle and nutrition guides: reviewed every 18–24 months.
  • Product and device reviews: reviewed whenever a significant new product version or study is released.

Each article displays its original publication date and the date it was last reviewed or updated, clearly visible near the top of the page.

Editorial Independence & Advertising

Natural Health One generates revenue through display advertising and affiliate marketing partnerships. Our editorial content is completely independent of our commercial relationships. Advertisers and affiliate partners have no influence over which topics we cover, how we cover them, or what conclusions we draw.

When a product review or recommendation article contains affiliate links, this is disclosed clearly at the top of that page. Our reviews are based on independent research, expert input, and objective criteria — never on payment from brands.

Corrections Policy

We take factual accuracy seriously. If an error is identified — whether by our editorial team, readers, or medical reviewers — we correct it promptly and transparently. Significant corrections are noted at the top of the affected article with a description of what was changed and why.

If you believe you have found an error or have a concern about any of our content, please contact our editorial team.

What We Will Never Publish

Our editorial standards explicitly prohibit the following:

  • Claims that any supplement, diet, or lifestyle intervention can "cure" or "reverse" diabetes without robust clinical evidence.
  • Content that discourages readers from taking prescribed medication without first consulting their doctor.
  • Unverified testimonials presented as medical evidence.
  • Content written or substantially influenced by a brand, manufacturer, or advertiser.
  • Misleading or alarmist headlines that do not accurately represent the content of the article.
