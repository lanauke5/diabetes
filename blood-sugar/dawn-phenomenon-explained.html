Dawn Phenomenon Explained

The dawn phenomenon is a natural rise in glucose before waking. It is common, and it is not your fault.

By NaturalHealthOne EditorsUpdated September 18, 20262 min read
Advertisement

What it actually is

A rise in glucose in the hours before waking, driven by a normal cycle of hormones that prepare the body for the day.

It happens in people without diabetes too. In diabetes, the rise is larger because insulin response is reduced.

Why it is so frustrating

You can do everything right and still wake to a higher reading than the night before.

This is a physiological pattern, not a failure of willpower or of your plan.

How it differs from other morning patterns

A late evening meal or insufficient overnight medication can also raise morning glucose, and they need different responses.

The only way to tell them apart is the pattern of context around the readings.

What can be done about it

A consistent evening routine, avoiding late meals, protecting sleep, and discussing medication timing with your clinician.

Never adjust medication yourself to address it.

Keep the next step safe

This article is educational and is not medical advice. Never adjust insulin or medication timing to address morning readings without your clinician. Discuss persistent high mornings with them so the cause can be identified. Read our full medical disclaimer.
