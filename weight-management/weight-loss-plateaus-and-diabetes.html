Weight Loss Plateaus And Diabetes

Category: Weight Management • By Editorial Team • Updated 2026-09-18 • 2 min read

Advertisement

Why plateaus happen

As body mass decreases, energy needs fall, so the same deficit produces a smaller effect.

Hunger often increases at the same time, which narrows the actual deficit further.

The plateau that is not a plateau

Fat loss can continue while weight holds, because activity adds muscle and water shifts.

Measurements and fit of clothing are often more informative than the scale during these periods.

What to actually do

Wait at least three weeks before changing anything, and review the whole pattern rather than the last reading.

If a change is needed, adjust activity or one eating habit, not everything at once.

When a plateau means something else

A plateau with worsening glucose, new symptoms, or fatigue is worth reporting rather than attributing to diet.

Readings provide information the scale cannot.

"Fat loss can continue while weight holds, because activity adds muscle and water shifts."

Bring these details to your next visit

  • Wait three weeks before changing a plateaued plan.
  • Use clothing fit and measurements, not just weight.
  • Change one variable at a time if needed.
  • Track glucose alongside weight for context.
  • Report fatigue or worsening readings promptly.
  • Review the whole month, not the last week.
